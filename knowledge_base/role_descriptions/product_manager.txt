ROLE: Product Manager
======================

ROLE OVERVIEW
-------------
A Product Manager (PM) owns the vision, strategy, and execution of a product or product area. They act as the connective tissue between engineering, design, marketing, sales, and customer success. A PM defines what to build, why to build it, and when, and is accountable for the product's success as measured by user and business outcomes. PMs are not people managers — they lead through influence and conviction.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
- Define and communicate a clear product vision and strategy aligned to business goals
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to deeply understand customer problems
- Build and maintain a prioritised product roadmap based on customer value, business impact, and feasibility
- Write clear product requirements documents (PRDs), epics, and user stories with acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with design to create and iterate on prototypes and user flows
- Work with engineering teams in agile/scrum to deliver features iteratively
- Define success metrics and OKRs for product initiatives and track them post-launch
- Conduct competitive analysis and monitor market trends to inform product decisions
- Manage stakeholder expectations across executives, customers, and internal teams
- Launch features and coordinate go-to-market activities with marketing and sales

IMPORTANT SKILLS
----------------
Product & Strategic Skills:
- Product discovery: user interviews, jobs-to-be-done, opportunity mapping
- Prioritisation frameworks: RICE, ICE, MoSCoW, Kano, Value vs. Effort
- Roadmapping tools: Aha!, Jira, ProductBoard, Trello
- Metrics definition: activation rate, retention, NPS, churn, DAU/MAU, conversion
- A/B testing and experimentation design
- Go-to-market planning: positioning, launch sequencing, channel strategy

Technical Fluency (not coding):
- Ability to read API documentation and understand technical trade-offs
- Understanding of system architecture at a conceptual level
- Familiarity with data analysis tools: SQL basics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Google Analytics

Soft Skills:
- Persuasion and influence without authority
- Structured thinking and clear written communication
- Empathy for users and for engineering/design teammates
- Comfort with ambiguity and rapid iteration

COMMON INTERVIEW FOCUS AREAS
------------------------------
1. Product Design & Strategy: "Design a product for X." Walk through problem definition, user research, personas, and feature prioritisation. Show structured thinking.
2. Prioritisation: Given 10 feature requests and limited engineering capacity, how do you decide? Know RICE, MoSCoW, and be ready to defend your choices.
3. Metrics & Analytics: What metrics would you track for feature Y? Define success, leading indicators, and guard rail metrics. Know funnel metrics.
4. Execution & Delivery: How do you work with engineering? How do you handle scope creep? What is your sprint ceremony cadence?
5. User Research: How do you validate a hypothesis? What research methods do you use and when?
6. Behavioural: Conflict with engineering, pushing back on executives, handling a failed launch.
7. Estimation & Case Studies: Market sizing, business case calculations, back-of-envelope reasoning.
8. Product Sense: "How would you improve [well-known product]?" Demonstrate taste, user empathy, and business acumen.
